What the estimates mean

Walk comparison = modeled PM₂.₅ at the next occurrence of your usual walk time versus the lowest mean across three hourly values spanning two daytime hours on that same local date. We assume 20 minutes outside. It does not estimate inhaled dose and excludes indoor infiltration, breathing rate, route differences, and personal health.

Commute estimates use Government of India vehicle emission factors and explicit default occupancy assumptions. A positive number means estimated CO₂ saved; a negative number means added. These are directional, not a personal carbon audit. Open the source factors ↗
